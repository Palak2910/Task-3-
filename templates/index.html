How it works

Seven stages, each independently verifiable, from pixels to an on-chain record.

Find faces in the uploaded image.

04

05

06

07

Blockchain verification

Retrieve and verify the recorded fingerprint and confirmation.

Technology stack

What this tool does and doesn't prove. Face recognition here measures visual similarity between faces, within a fixed threshold — it does not confirm a person's real-world identity. Blockchain registration provides a tamper-evident timestamp for an image fingerprint; it proves that a specific hash existed at a specific time and hasn't changed since, not that the underlying face match was correct. A result of NOT VERIFIED does not mean an image is fake — it means no sufficiently similar, on-chain-confirmed match was found within this tool's configured threshold. Treat every result as one signal among many, not as a definitive judgment.
